# Idea: Body Awareness / Self-Regulation

A capstone idea for [[senior-capstone]]. **Problem:** many useful skills involving the
body, attention, stress, and habits aren't formally taught. People have limited
awareness of things happening in their own bodies (breathing, posture, stress,
recovery) even though that info could help them change behavior. **Vision:** something
that builds body awareness / teaches self-regulation.

- **Target audience:** athletes, students, people interested in health/wellness and
  human performance.
- **Why it fits her:** deeply personal — connects to her own work on
  [[nervous-system-regulation]] and being more in control of her conscious mind. Great
  candidate for wearables/sensors/haptics + physical computing from [[capstone-skills]].
- **Fit vs. [[capstone-goals]]:** strong on priority #1 (genuine personal interest → pride)
  and can be very real (priority #2). Likely the best hardware+software+meaning combo of
  her ideas. Open question: scope it tightly (one behavior, e.g. breath or posture) so
  it's doable in a semester and fully understandable (priority #3).

## Open questions for research (see [[capstone-research-log]])
- Which single signal is most tractable + impactful (breathing, posture, HRV/stress)?
- What sensors/wearables measure it well and affordably?
- What do existing biofeedback / posture / breathing products do, and where's the gap?
