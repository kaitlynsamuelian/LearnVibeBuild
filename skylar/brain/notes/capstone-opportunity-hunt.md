# Capstone Opportunity Hunt

The brief for Skylar's broad search for STRONG capstone opportunities for Kaitlyn —
beyond the ideas she already has (see [[senior-capstone]]). Goal: surface fresh,
well-scoped problems/opportunities that would make a great capstone. Findings land in
`capstone-opportunities.md` (created by `scripts/explore.sh`).

## The philosophy (how to hunt)
- **Problems, not products.** Frame everything as a problem + audience ("How might we…"),
  not a pre-decided gadget. Discover whether a solution is actually the best fit.
- **Hunt the overlooked.** The best project is probably hiding in *"things people have
  accepted as normal even though they're badly designed"* — the everyday "ugh" moments:
  putting clothes somewhere to dry, digging through a makeup drawer, moving three things
  to reach one, forgetting what's in the back of a closet, five speakers that can't play
  together, cleaning a weird corner with a toothbrush, umbrellas that fail in any wind, etc.
- **Validate the need.** For each opportunity, check what already exists and whether people
  actually complain / care (real demand signal), not assumed demand.
- **Right scope.** One semester, iterative, not rushed. Define a realistic MVP.
- **Her intersection.** Favor **physical product design × technology × real human need**
  over screen-only apps. Should use her [[capstone-skills]] AND grow the ones she wants
  (physical prototyping, product/industrial design, user research, embedded/hardware,
  networking, local AI). Judge against her ★5 in [[capstone-goals]].

## Domains she's drawn to (fair game, not limits)
Education, privacy & security, experimental games, sustainability, fashion, immersive
environments, community building, biomedical, accessibility, wellness/human performance,
small-space living, everyday-object organization.

## Top-tier directions already surfaced (build past these, don't just repeat)
- **Physical ↔ digital closet / "collection intelligence"** — making physical collections
  (clothes, then shoes/makeup) digitally aware; the pain is cataloging/maintenance, not
  "AI picks outfits." (crowded app space = signal the real problem isn't solved)
- **Universal speaker / audio bridge** — combine already-owned, cross-brand, cross-era
  audio into one synced system (see [[idea-speaker-bridge]]).
- **Student intention-vs-behavior system** — not another planner; the gap between knowing
  what to do, doing it, and understanding why days go the way they do.
- **Cross-sensory / sensory translation** (accessibility + new experiences) — info from one
  sense translated to another (sound→touch/light, color→sound, proximity→haptics).
- **The object AirTags don't work for** — frequently-lost valuables that still can't carry a
  tracker (thin/washable/flexible/no-battery/integrated).
- **Small-space clothing drying** ([[idea-drying-rack]]) — stays alive; don't let it dominate.

## What a good output looks like
~8–12 opportunities, each with: problem ("how might we…") + audience; what already exists
(with source links); the gap; demand signal (real vs assumed); one-semester MVP scope;
why it fits Kaitlyn (skills used + grown); and a rating against her ★5. Then a ranked
top tier and an honest recommendation.
