About this shelf

This is my studio space for Learn, Vibe, Build, a class where I’ll be creating artifacts throughout the semester to grow my skills, experiment with AI, and learn through building.

Currently: 3 artifacts. Two are guides I created to better understand Cursor and Claude as tools and to keep as resources moving forward. The third, Audit Decoder, is a tool that takes a PDF export of your degree audit from Buff Portal and turns its messy, confusing text into a clear, digestible guide to your current degree progress.

New artifacts will land here as the semester goes on.

A student project for class. Not affiliated with Cursor / Anysphere, Anthropic, or the University of Colorado. Always confirm degree audits with a human advisor.
