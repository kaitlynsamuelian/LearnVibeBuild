A review of the inspiration-log framing caught an overclaim: “every renter-safe option is too weak for wet laundry.” That is not true.

Polder Mountain Lock Dryer is over-the-door, no drill, 40 lb official, 16 ft of line. Wall-mount is optional.

Keep this sentence instead: wall racks that hold a lot still want studs. Renter-safe racks that hold a lot take the door or the floor. Opportunity = high-capacity + collapsible + renter-safe without occupying the floor or taking over a door.

Do not put “every renter-safe option is too weak” on a slide.
