Updated 29 September 2026: the lab runs two tracks now

After the sounding board with Aidan Chopra, install modularity plus reconfigurable geometry is no longer the locked concept. It is one of two open questions.

  • Track one, section R. Is reconfiguration even wanted? Would anyone actually change the rack’s shape or mount per load, or does that add a chore to a chore? The goal is easy and passive.
  • Track two, section A. Can the rack itself dry faster via airflow? Air distributed through the structure, hollow or perforated rods, holes aimed at the garment. Not a fan on the floor.

The two tracks run in parallel. The airflow prototype does not wait on the reconfiguration answer. At the end we pick whichever problem is the more interesting opportunity and that becomes the capstone. See chopra-feedback.html.

The old install tickets (P, I, L, G) stay OPEN. Nothing has closed them, and whichever track wins the rack still has to stand up under a wet load. Attachments stay parked, unless the custom clip-for-rod experiment comes back from the airflow track.

Run order

  1. Six loads, observed and photographed.
  2. User talks and the survey.
  3. Reconfiguration evidence, assembled and answered.
  4. First airflow prototype (runs in parallel with 1 to 3).
  5. Pick which problem is the capstone.

How to ask for a run

  • One ticket: “Run ticket A4 from the agenda.”
  • One section: “Run every OPEN ticket in section R.”
  • Nightly: “Pick 2 OPEN tickets marked NIGHTLY.”
  • Whole pass: “Work through every OPEN ticket in order.”

After a run, make a new card for the findings and mark that ticket DONE in the markdown. If you cannot find something, write NOT FOUND rather than filling the gap. Bench tests go on the Experiments shelf, one card per test. How to file: experiments/how-to-file.html.

Added 30 September 2026: the premium precedent pass

A second pass ran on 30 September, aimed at one question: how can a drying system actively improve air-drying through airflow, materials, geometry, or integrated mechanisms, while still fitting naturally into a living space? Cost was deliberately left out of scope, so this pass records no bill of materials and no target price. Six named precedents were filed (dyson-air-multiplier, samsung-airdresser, lg-styler, drying-pod, heated-airer-plus, perforated-duct) plus a seventh on living with the object (live-with-it), with the synthesis in premium-airflow-precedents.html and the assignment framing in change-from-last-week.html. Section A gained tickets A11 to A16, mostly bench variables the precedents handed us: slot width, hole-area ratio and taper, partial enclosure, the hanger as part of the air path, motion without a motor, and a noise benchmark. Nothing here closes R1. The six loads are still the primary evidence and the dual track still stands.
