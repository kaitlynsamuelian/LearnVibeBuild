Polder Mountain Lock Dryer (DRY-9816). Over-the-door, no drill, 40 lb official, 16 ft of line. Optional wall-mount screws. About $35–57.

What it proves

Installation can be reconsidered. People will hang a rack on a door if the floor is gone.

What it does not do

It only lives on a door. If the next apartment has no good door, or you need the door free, you buy a different product.

Link to our concept

This is the “install can change” precedent. It is not the whole system. Do not describe it as a wall mount.
