Brabantia WallFix is a fold-out wall drying rack. High drying length. It stores flat against a wall and opens when you need it.

What it proves

A rack’s form can change between stored and in use. Geometry is already a product idea, just usually as “fold away,” not “taller for pants.”

What it does not do

It still commits you to one install (a solid wall, not typical rental drywall). It is not renter-safe in the Polder sense.

Link to our concept

Use it as the configuration / stored-vs-open precedent, not as proof we can mount on any wall.
