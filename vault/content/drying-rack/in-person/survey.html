Form intro text

I am a senior at CU Boulder working on a capstone about how people dry laundry at home. This takes about three minutes. There are no right answers and I am not selling anything. If you air-dry even occasionally, your answers are useful.

One demographic question, handled carefully

Aidan specifically asked for more women and more people who hang-dry a wide range of garments, so we do need to be able to segment. Add at the end, optional, with a prefer-not-to-say option:

  • Gender: Woman / Man / Non-binary / Prefer to self-describe / Prefer not to say
  • Age: 18 to 22 / 23 to 29 / 30 to 44 / 45 to 64 / 65+

Keep these last so they do not frame the rest of the survey, and keep them optional.

Notes on how this is built

  • Question 9 is the quiet test of the airflow hypothesis. If people go back and poke at a load, drying is uneven, and uneven drying is an air-delivery problem.
  • Question 11 is the reconfiguration test, and it is deliberately worded as a memory rather than a preference. “Would you like an adjustable rack” gets a yes from everyone and means nothing. “Have you ever wished” asks for an actual moment.
  • Question 7 tests whether folding away after every load is even a behaviour. Our whole stored-versus-open pitch assumed it was.
  • Question 10 is free text on purpose. If speed beats footprint here in people’s own words, that is a real finding. See laundry-mode.html for the language to expect.
  • Do not add a question that describes our concept. Nothing in this survey should teach the respondent what answer we want.
