The week's brief is given in class and added here after class. Every week has the same shape (10 points):
* Artifact — make something someone else can try, read, watch, or use. Small, unfinished, or strange is fine.
* Reflection — at least one paragraph in your own words: what you tried, what happened, what you learned. Written by you, not by AI.
* Studio share — on your demo week, share what you're working on; on off weeks, listen and give real feedback.
Submit a link, file, or text with your reflection. Disclose any material AI tools you used. Posting on the Learn Vibe Build site is fine too — also drop the link here so it's on your record.
