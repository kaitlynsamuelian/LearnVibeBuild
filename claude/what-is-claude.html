What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant built by a company called Anthropic. "Claude" is also an umbrella for a small family of related things. Let's meet each one and see how they fit together.

The main thing

Claude, the AI assistant

When people say "Claude," they usually mean the assistant you chat with at claude.ai (or in the Claude desktop and phone apps). You type a message; Claude replies. It's a conversation.

You can ask it almost anything: explain a concept, draft a message, summarize a PDF, brainstorm ideas, analyze a spreadsheet, plan a trip, or help with code. It remembers the conversation, so you can refine and follow up naturally.

  • No coding or setup — just type in plain language.
  • You can attach files and images for Claude to read.
  • Great for writing, thinking, learning, and everyday tasks.

The builder

Claude Code, the agentic coding tool

Claude Code is a separate tool that puts Claude to work directly on your computer. Instead of just talking, it can read your project's files, make edits, run commands, and check its own work — looping until a task is done. That "takes actions on its own" quality is what people mean by agentic.

It mainly runs in the terminal (a text-command window), but it also works inside code editors, a desktop app, and even on the web. It's aimed at building and changing software.

Same Claude intelligence, different job. The assistant chats; Claude Code does. There's a whole Claude Code chapter when you're ready.

The Claude models — the "brains" you can choose

Under the hood, Claude runs on AI models. Anthropic offers a few, named by tiers, so you can trade off capability, speed, and cost. You'll see these names in a dropdown when you use Claude.

Default

Sonnet

The balanced everyday model — fast and smart. It's the default on the Free and Pro plans, and where most people should start.

Flagship

Opus

The powerful workhorse for the hardest problems — complex reasoning and long, involved coding tasks.

Fastest

Haiku

The quickest, lightest, lowest-cost model. Best when speed and volume matter more than maximum depth.

Frontier

Fable

Anthropic's most capable widely-available model, reserved for the most demanding reasoning and agentic work.

Simple rule: start with Sonnet. Move up to Opus or Fable for the toughest tasks, or down to Haiku when you want speed and low cost. As a beginner, the default is perfect.

Model names carry a version number (for example "Sonnet 4.5" or "Opus 5") and Anthropic ships new versions often. The tier names — Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, Fable — are the stable part. Check the docs for today's exact lineup.

Who makes Claude?

Claude is built by Anthropic, an AI safety and research company. Claude is named in the spirit of making AI feel approachable and human-friendly.

Anthropic puts a strong emphasis on making Claude helpful, honest, and harmless — which is part of why Claude tends to be careful, explains its reasoning, and pushes back when something seems off.

A quick note on trust

Claude is impressively capable, but like all AI it can occasionally be wrong or "make things up" (called a hallucination). Treat it as a brilliant, fast assistant — not an infallible oracle.

Double-check anything important, and ask Claude to show its reasoning or sources when it matters.
